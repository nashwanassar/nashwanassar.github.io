Privacy

Last updated 17 November 2026

What this site collects

This is my personal portfolio. I use two analytics tools, Microsoft Clarity and Google Analytics, to understand how visitors use it, so I can improve it.

Clarity records:

  • usage statistics, such as pages viewed, device, browser and approximate location
  • heatmaps of where people click and how far they scroll
  • session recordings of clicks, scrolls and mouse movement

Clarity masks sensitive content by default.

Google Analytics records usage statistics, such as pages viewed, how you reached the site, device, browser and approximate location. It doesn’t record your screen, and this site doesn’t use its advertising features.

I don't use this data to identify you.

Microsoft’s and Google’s role

Microsoft collects this data through cookies and similar technologies, and also uses it as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Google collects its data through cookies and similar technologies, and uses it as described in the Google Privacy Policy and in how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

By using this site, you agree that I, Microsoft and Google can collect and use this data.

Visitors from the EEA, UK and Switzerland

This site doesn’t ask for consent, so for visits from these regions neither tool sets analytics cookies. Clarity runs without cookies, and Google Analytics sends only cookieless signals that don’t identify your browser.

Opting out

Both tools on this site honour the Global Privacy Control signal, which some browsers and extensions can send for you. You can also use Google’s opt-out browser add-on, or block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
