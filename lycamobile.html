01

The challenge

One brand, a different experience in every market

Lycamobile is one of the largest telecoms in the UK and European markets. As part of IBM’s digital transformation team, we worked with Lycamobile’s Head of CX, the CX team and the CMO to understand the business requirements and aspirations, and the challenges and motivations of customers in each country.

The experience was inconsistent from one market to the next, each country’s regulations changed the user flow, and there was little feedback on the customer experience.

What we found

We reviewed what customers said about Lycamobile online, interviewed end users about their experience and pain points, and ran working sessions with the Head of CX, the CX team and the CMO. Four themes came up again and again:

  • An inconsistent experience across markets
  • A heavy customer service load, which led to a bad experience
  • A lack of transparency
  • Different regulations in each country

02

My role

Leading the UX design squad

As UX Lead, I led the UX design squad and worked with the CX and marketing teams to define the problems and design a better experience. We built different versions of the Lycamobile platform, its product catalogue and its plans for Australia, Europe and the US, based on each market’s needs and regulations.

How we worked

  • Discovery: online reviews, user interviews and working sessions with the CX team and the CMO
  • Ideation: user types and user stories
  • Experimentation: wireframes, prototypes and feedback

03

What we designed

Home and purchase journeys for every kind of customer

We designed for two splits in the audience: new versus returning customers, and national versus international plans. The home page had a version for each of them.

Buying a plan

Plans came in different types and durations, from 7 and 28 days to 3, 6 and 12 months, which made buying more complex. We split the plan picker into short-term and long-term plans, for new and returning customers alike.
